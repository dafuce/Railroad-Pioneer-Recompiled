In this repo i am messing around with the PC game Railroad Pioneer, made with the Nebula Device engine in 2001 by KRITZELKRATZ 3000 studio in Germany and originally published by JoWood Europe.
I had the game as a child in CD-ROM and i found it back on steam published by THQ Nordic Mobile in 2019.
Today in 2026 the AI tools are making decompilation of many games reasonably easy and I thought to give it a try on this one.
I recently played again the whole game and found some bugs on the latest version of the game published on steam, the 1.09. Since this is clearly a case of abandonware, i am trying to fix them myself and i will post about my wins or losses.
On the other hand, I think some aspects of the game could use some rebalancing to make it more enjoyable. Since those were not intended by the developer, I see fit to keep two branches updated, one with the "unintended" bugs fixed, and another one with my own tweaks.
I am not too sure yet about the legality of distributing a modified version or a modifier of the game so I won't push this repo, as I stated I do not claim any rights to the game and all parties have been mentioned as authors or publishers.
This repo is only meant for nostalgic fans of the game as myself who have an original copy of the game or one purchased from Steam, I will not post all the resources to recompile the game and play it for free.
English is not my first language and I am not reviewing any of my writing with AI crap, I keep it real :)
